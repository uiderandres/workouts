Frequency

Legs trained 3x/week (Mon full, Wed light, Fri full) — enough frequency for continued growth without overlap fatigue. Upper body sits at 2x/week, kept at maintenance.

Spacing

No two full leg sessions land back-to-back: Mon → Tue upper → Wed light touch → Thu upper → Fri full → full weekend rest.

Progression

Once a rep range feels easy, add a loaded backpack or slow the eccentric further (5-6s down) — intensity without added noise.
